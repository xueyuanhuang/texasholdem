Privacy

Updated September 13, 2026

Poker is a game-recording tool. This notice explains how account information, personal records, and club records are used.

Account information

When you sign in with Google, Google provides Supabase, our authentication service, with an account identifier, email address, and basic profile information such as your name and photo. These are used to create your account, authenticate you, and identify club members. The app does not request access to Gmail messages, contacts, or Google Drive files. Email-code sign-in is also handled by Supabase.

Game and club records

The app stores records on your device. After sign-in, personal records sync to Supabase. Creating a club copies your personal records into the club. The original personal records remain; the two sets of records are then updated independently.

Approved members can view all history in their club. The manager can see member emails, approve membership and player links, and grant or revoke game-management access. Authorized organizers can modify club games. Previously viewed or exported copies cannot be recalled remotely.

Services and account management

The website is hosted by Cloudflare Pages. Supabase provides authentication and cloud storage; Google provides identity verification when you select Google sign-in. These services also have their own privacy notices.

You can sign out from the account menu in Settings. Contact us to request a copy of your records. Signing out does not delete cloud records. To request account deletion, ask about your information, or raise a privacy question, contact xue_yuanhuang@163.com. Changes to shared club history involving other members may need coordination with the club manager.
